## When you need a commercial licence

The GPL lets you run the tool for any purpose, including inside a company, and
share the captures it produces. A commercial licence is meant for uses the GPL
does not allow or that you would rather not do under its terms, for example:

- shipping mcx-anonymizer, or a modified version, inside a closed-source
  product or appliance;
- linking the `mcxanon` library into proprietary software;
- offering the tool to customers without passing on the source code and the GPL
  rights.

If you are not sure whether your use needs one, ask us.

## What a commercial licence can include

- the right to embed or redistribute the tool without the GPL obligations;
- support and agreed response times;
- rule sets and features developed to order, for example for vendor-specific
  headers or further interfaces;
- help with validating the tool on your own captures.
